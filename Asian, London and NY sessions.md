1. The 18:00 Reversal (Asian Session Setup)
Context: At 18:00 EST, the Asian session begins. In ICT theory, this is often the "Accumulation" phase.

The Reversal: You are looking for the market to establish a high or low for the day (a "reversal" point). If the market sweeps liquidity or hits a key Higher Timeframe (HTF) level during the Asian session, this reversal point effectively sets the "anchor" for the rest of the day's move.

2. The 01:00 Expansion (London Killzone)
Context: 01:00 EST marks the start of the London Killzone.

The Expansion: This is where the "Manipulation" or "Expansion" phase usually occurs. If the price "expands away" from the 18:00 reversal point during this window, it confirms that the institutional algorithm has picked a direction.

Why it matters: If the market expands strongly away from the Asian range, it suggests that the "smart money" is positioning for a directional move rather than remaining in consolidation.

3. The 08:00 Continuation (New York Killzone)
Context: 08:00 EST is the start of the New York AM Killzone.

The Continuation: By this time, the "London move" has likely exhausted its initial thrust. The 08:00 window is frequently used by the algorithm to offer a continuation setup.

The Logic: If the price trends throughout the London session, the New York open often provides a retracement (a "discount" or "premium" entry) into a Fair Value Gap (FVG) or Order Block, allowing for a continuation of the London trend into the afternoon.

How to use this in your Edge
You can think of this as a "Session Confirmation" filter. If you see this pattern unfolding, you avoid fighting the trend.

If you see the 01:00 expansion: You now have a "Bias" for the day. You are no longer looking for reversals; you are looking for entries in the direction of the London move.

Wait for the 08:00 window: In
stead of chasing the London expansion at 4:00 AM, you wait for the 08:00 NY opening to provide the "re-entry" opportunity.

Summary for your Trading Plan:

"If the market establishes a swing (reversal) at 18:00 and shows strong displacement (expansion) at 01:00, I will hold a directional bias for the day and wait for the 08:00 New York window to find a high-probability continuation entry."

Why ICT Says "Seek Continuation"
According to ICT, if the 01:00 London window actively reverses the Asian range, that 01:00 move is the "True Direction" of the day. * The Trap: Retail traders see the market break the Asian high or low at 01:00 and try to trade the breakout.

The Institutional Reality: The algorithm is sweeping that liquidity to fuel a move in the opposite direction. Once that sweep is complete, the market has "cleared the decks" and is free to trend.

The 08:00 Entry: By the time the New York session opens, the "big move" from London is often consolidating or pulling back slightly. This pull-back is the professional opportunity to enter (the "Continuation") because the institutional direction has already been confirmed by the London reversal.

This specific scenario describes what ICT refers to as a Liquidity Sweep followed by a Trend Day development. When the 01:00 London window reverses the 18:00 Asian range, it is considered a high-probability institutional setup.
