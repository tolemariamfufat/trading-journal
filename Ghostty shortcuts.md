The Real Linux Split Shortcuts
Ctrl + Shift + O : Split the window Vertically (creates a new pane to the right).

Ctrl + Shift + E : Split the window Horizontally (creates a new pane below).

Ctrl + Shift + W : Close the active pane.

Moving Between Panes
To move your typing focus between your open panels, press:

Ctrl + Super + [ (Go to the previous pane)

Ctrl + Super + ] (Go to the next pane)

(Note: Super is the "Windows" key on your keyboard).

Scrollback Search / Global Search
Verdict: Yes, this works perfectly.

As of Ghostty version 1.3, native scrollback search is fully implemented for Linux.

How it works: When you press Ctrl + Shift + F on your system, a clean search bar will pop up in the window. You can type any string, and it will highlight all occurrences throughout your scrollback history.

Navigation: You can navigate through the results smoothly using Enter (to go forward) and Shift + Enter (to go backward).
