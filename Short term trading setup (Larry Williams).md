1. The "Trading Mechanism" for Staying In
What is the "mechanism" that allows you to stay in a winning trade as long as possible? It is the combination of your 60-day Market Structure and the Opening Price Rules you just decoded:

The Anchor: You use the 20/40/60-day study to find the "Draw on Liquidity" (the target).

The Shield: You use the Opening Price (Rules 2 & 4) as your invalidation. If price stays on the "right side" of the open, you have no reason to exit.

The Logic: If it is a "Large-Range Day," it must close at the high/low. Therefore, the most mathematical way to trade is to enter near the open (after the Judas Swing) and do nothing until the end of the day.

2. The Great Irony of Short-Term Trading
Williams points out a brilliant contradiction: To be a successful short-term trader, you must actually think long-term (relative to the day).

The Mistake: Most traders exit as soon as they see a small profit because they fear the "short-term swings" Williams says are unpredictable.

The Professional Way: You accept that the "zig-zags" during the day are noise. You stay in because you are waiting for the Explosive Move (Distribution) to reach its natural conclusion at the High or Low of the day.
