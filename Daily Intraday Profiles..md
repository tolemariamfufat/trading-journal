## 1. The Classic Buy/Sell Day (London Trend Profile)

This is what you observed on GBPUSD.
- **Behavior:** Price creates a small "Judas Swing" (manipulation) very early, often before 2:00 AM, or simply pulls back to an FVG/Order Block and then trends for the rest of the day.
- **Key Detail:** The Low of the Day (for a buy) or High of the Day (for a sell) is usually set in London between 2:00 AM and 5:00 AM.
    ## 2. London Swing to Z-Day (Consolidation Profile)
- **Behavior:** London creates a massive trend move, but by the time New York opens, the market has "run out of gas."
- **Result:** The afternoon (PM session) turns into a "Z-Day," which is essentially a tight, sideways consolidation. This happens when the market reaches its Daily target early.
## 3. London Swing to New York Reversal

- **Behavior:** London creates the "apparent" trend for the day. However, once New York opens (8:30 AM), price hits a Higher Timeframe (HTF) level and reverses completely, often taking out the London High/Low.
- **Common trigger:** High-impact news at 8:30 AM or 10:00 AM NY time.
## 4. Small Range / Consolidation Day

- **Behavior:** The market stays within the Asian Range for almost the entire day.
- **Why it happens:** Usually occurs before a major news event (like an FOMC meeting or NFP Friday) or when the Higher Timeframe is in "equilibrium" and has no reason to move.
## 5. Seek and Destroy (The "Expansion of Both Sides")
- **Behavior:** This is the most dangerous profile. London takes out the Asian High **and** the Asian Low. Then New York does the same to the London High and Low.
- **The Trap:** It’s designed to stop out both buyers and sellers before the real move happens the next day.
## 6. Consolidation Raid on News (The News Profile)
- **Behavior:** The market remains in a tight consolidation during both Asia and London. All the "manipulation" and "expansion" are packed into 5 minutes during a news release (like CPI).

Daily Profile
The 6 Daily Profiles
The video categorizes every trading day into one of these six templates:

P1 (Trending/Continuation): Asia trends in one direction, London expands in that same direction, and New York continues the trend. There is no manipulation.

P1B (Delayed Continuation): Asia sets a direction, but London stays quiet and consolidates inside Asia’s range. New York then "breaks out" and continues Asia's original direction.

P2 (The Classic Sweep/Judas Swing): London runs the high or low of the Asia range to grab liquidity and then reverses. New York follows London’s reversal direction.

P3 (Seek & Destroy/Double Sweep): Both Asia and London consolidate in a tight range. The 8:00 AM candle sweeps both the high and the low before New York picks a sharp direction.

P4 (New York Reversal with Sweep): Asia and London are aligned (both moving up or down), but New York sweeps the London extreme and reverses the entire trend.

P4B (New York Reversal without Sweep): Similar to P4, but New York reverses immediately at the open without a "fake-out" or liquidity sweep of the London high/low.
