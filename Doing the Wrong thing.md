This is the "Black Hole" of Chapter 14. Larry Williams titles this section **"Doing the Wrong Thing... It's So Easy, Isn't It?"** because he wants to highlight that in trading, your natural human instincts are perfectly designed to make you fail.

He argues that the "Right Thing" in trading usually feels physically and emotionally "Wrong," while the "Wrong Thing" feels safe and comfortable.
### 1. The Comfort of the Crowd

Larry points out that "Doing the Wrong Thing" is easy because it’s usually what everyone else is doing.

- **The Error:** Buying when the market is screaming higher and the "Ask" is being hit with massive velocity _at the end of a move_.
    
- **Why it’s Easy:** You feel safe because you see other people making money. Your brain says, "The trend is strong, I should leap!"
    
- **The Reality:** By the time it feels "easy" and "safe" to buy, the **Institutional Sponsorship** is already looking for the exit. You are the liquidity they need to sell into.
### 2. The Pain of the "Right Thing"

Conversely, the "Right Thing" is hard because it feels like suicide.

- **The Action:** Buying a **Smash Day** or an **Oops! Gap** when the market has been crashing for three days.
    
- **Why it’s Hard:** Every cell in your body is screaming at you to wait for a "clear signal" or "stabilization."
    
- **Larry’s Point:** If you wait for the market to look stable, the **Inertia** has already carried the price 2% higher. You missed the "Honey."
### 3. Why the Specialist Wants You to Do the "Easy" Thing

Larry reminds us that the **Specialist** (the Market Maker) is the one making it look "Easy."

- They create the "Velocity" at the highs to tempt you in.
    
- They create the "Fear" at the lows to force you out.
    
- **The Lesson:** If the trade feels easy, check your **Yardstick**. You are likely being lured into a **Specialist's Trap.**
### 4. Summary: The Discipline of the "Difficult"

Larry concludes that if you want to be a professional, you must learn to **distrust your feelings.**

1. **If it feels safe:** You are likely late.
    
2. **If it feels scary:** Your setup is likely at its highest probability.
    
3. **The Solution:** Stop being a human; be a **Machine**. Use your **3-bar swing** and your **Money Management** to automate the "Hard" thing so it becomes your "Standard Operating Procedure."

It really is so easy to do the wrong thing, isn't it? It's as natural as breathing. That’s why 90% of traders fail—they just do what feels natural.
