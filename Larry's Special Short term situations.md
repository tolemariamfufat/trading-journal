
1. The "Month-End" and "First of the Month" Moves
One of Larry’s favorite "special situations" is the flow of money from pension funds and institutional investors.

The Logic: At the end of the month, funds rebalance. On the first day of the month, new capital is injected into the market.

The Trade: He identified that the S&P 500 has a massive bullish bias during the last 2 days and first 3 days of the month.

ICT Connection: This is Institutional Funding Flow. The algorithm is "programmed" to provide liquidity for these massive capital injections, creating a predictable bullish expansion.

2. The "Friday Close" / "Monday Open" Situation
Larry observed that how the market closes on a Friday often dictates the "Inertia" for the following Monday.

The Situation: If the market closes at the absolute high of the week on Friday, it suggests the "Specialists" are not finished buying.

The Result: This often leads to a Monday "Oops!" Gap or a continuation of the trend.

ICT Connection: This is the study of the Weekly Profile. A Friday close at the high suggests a "Large Range Bullish Tuesday" or "Wednesday" profile is coming next week.

3. Holiday "Special Situations"
Larry found that the days leading up to major US holidays (like July 4th, Thanksgiving, and Christmas) have a nearly 70-80% probability of being bullish.

The Logic: Large sellers leave the floor, and the remaining "Inertia" is positive.

The Strategy: He looks for Smash Day buy signals specifically during these holiday windows because the "Friction" (selling pressure) is at its lowest point of the year.
