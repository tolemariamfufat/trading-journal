The DE30 Cash Open Reference Windows
The main cash equity exchange in Germany has official operating hours that create the clean "daily gap" footprints.

1. The DE30 Regular Trading Hours (RTH) Closing Time
The Time: 17:30 CET (Central European Time)  

**In NY Time: 11:30 AM NY Time

What it is: This is when the Frankfurt cash market officially shuts its books for the day. Price "settles" here.

2. The DE30 Regular Trading Hours (RTH) Opening Time
The Time: 09:00 CET (Central European Time)  

**In NY Time: 3:00 AM NY Time

What it is: This is when the official European opening bell rings and the cash market re-opens.
