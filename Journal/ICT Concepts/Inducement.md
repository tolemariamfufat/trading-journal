## The Master Note: Inducement (IDM) & Liquidity Engineering

### 1. The Core Philosophy
- **Inducement is the "Fuel":** Market price does not move sustainably without first "engineering" a trap.
- **The Trap:** Smart Money creates a short-term high or low (the IDM) to trick retail traders into entering early.
- **The Sweep:** The market must take the liquidity (stop losses) sitting at that IDM before the real move can occur.
### 2. The Mechanical Definition of IDM
- **IDM = The Most Recent Valid Pullback.**
- **The Candle Rule:** A pullback is ONLY valid if a candle breaks and trades beyond the high/low of the previous candle.
- **Inside Bars = Noise:** Candles that stay within the range of a previous candle do not create IDM.
- **Fractal Nature:** For every level of trend (Daily, 15M, 1M), an IDM exists. Confusion decreases as you move to the Higher Timeframe (HTF).
### 3. The Structural Confirmation
- **Confirming the High/Low:** A Higher High (in an uptrend) is **NOT** confirmed just by breaking the previous peak. It is only officially confirmed once the price retraces and **sweeps the IDM**.
- **Valid Trend Continuation:** A "Break of Structure" (BOS) is only high-probability if the price has already cleared its "liquidity debt" by taking the IDM.
### 4. IDM vs. Retracement vs. Mitigation
- **IDM Sweep:** A shallow grab of the most recent short-term liquidity. Often leads to an immediate move to a new high.
- **Deep Retracement:** When the market passes the IDM to reach deeper into **Discount** zones (Order Blocks/FVGs).
- **Mitigation:** The actual act of price "filling" an untapped Order Block _after_ the IDM has been cleared.
### 5. The "Veil" of Retail Tools (Fibonacci)
- **The Illusion:** Retail Fibonacci levels (38.2%, 50%, 61.8%) often act as a veil that hides the IDM.
- **The Truth:** Smart Money allows price to stall at Fib levels to build a "cluster" of stops, which they then sweep to hit the IDM.
- **The Rule:** Ignore the Fib "touch." Wait for the **Sweep** of the IDM sitting near those levels
### 6. The Triple Pillar (Summary of Synthesis)
A high-probability trade requires the collision of three dimensions at the IDM
1. **Structure (The Where):** 15M Dealing Range and confirmed highs/lows.
2. **Liquidity (The What):** Dynamic Liquidity (the trail of stop losses left by pullbacks).
3. **Time (The When):** The sweep must occur during a **Killzone** (London or New York Open).
