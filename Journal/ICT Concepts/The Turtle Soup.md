### The Turtle Soup (Liquidity Raid)
- This is a reversal model used to "fade" a breakout. It’s perfect for when the market is ranging or hitting a major Daily/Weekly level.
- The Window: London Open or NY Open.
- The Setup: Price sweeps a major Liquidity level (Previous Day High or Low) by 10–20 pips and immediately gets rejected.
- Entry: You enter as soon as the price moves back inside the old range (you don't always wait for an FVG here).
- Stop Loss: Just past the wick of the sweep.
- Take Profit: The "Internal Liquidity" (the nearest FVG on the opposite side).
