### 1. The Time Window (The 60-Minute "Killzone")
The Silver Bullet operates within a very specific 60-minute window: **10:00 AM to 11:00 AM EST**. This period is viewed as a high-probability time for the market to complete its morning objective [[02:28:04](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tub1JHNOIB0&t=8884)].
### 2. Market Context: Continuation
The setup is typically not a standalone reversal trade; it is a **continuation** of the logic established during the London session or the early New York session (8:30 AM – 10:00 AM). If the bias for the day has already been outlined by those earlier sessions, the Silver Bullet acts as the "final push" to reach the target.
### 3. The Draw on Liquidity (The Target)
For the Silver Bullet to work, there must be a clear **Draw on Liquidity**. This is the destination where the price is "drawn" to. Common targets mentioned include:
- **NWOG (New Week Opening Gap):** A price level from the weekend opening that the market wants to revisit.
- **FVG (Fair Value Gap):** An imbalance in the market. If you are bullish, you are aiming for a FVG above the current market price [[02:25:31](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tub1JHNOIB0&t=8731)].
- **Old Highs/Lows:** Liquidity resting above previous peaks or below previous troughs
### 4. The Catalyst: The First FVG
The mechanical trigger for the trade is the **first Fair Value Gap (FVG)** that forms on a lower time frame (typically the 1-minute or 5-minute chart) between 10:00 AM and 11:00 AM.
- Once this gap forms in the direction of your bias, it acts as the **catalyst** for the move.
- The market is expected to reach into or through this gap and then expand rapidly toward the Draw on Liquidity.
### 5. Expected Outcome: The 5-Handle Run
The objective of this specific model is modest and highly consistent: a **minimum 5-handle run** in the S&P 500 (ES) or similar indices. By focusing on a rule-based entry at a specific time with a clear target, the model aims to remove the "guessing" and emotional turmoil often found in discretionary trading [[02:24:25](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tub1JHNOIB0&t=8665)].
