### **1. Session Timing & Core Characteristics**

- **London Timing:** The session officially spans **2:00–5:00 AM**, but the high-probability **"sweet spot" is 2:00–4:00 AM**.
- **False Breakouts:** You should expect a high frequency of false breakouts. This is not an error in the market but a **natural characteristic** of the London session’s liquidity engineering.
### **2. Higher Time Frame (HTF) Framework**

- **The Importance of W1 & D1:** Trading London without referring to the Weekly and Daily charts makes your trade **"blurred."** Success is rooted in understanding where price is coming from on these HTFs.
- **W1 Anticipation:** On Monday, do not react; instead, **anticipate the next weekly candle**. Determine if it will expand up or down by looking for inefficiencies (Fair Value Gaps) or stops. This establishes your **bias** and frame of reference.
- **D1 Correlation:** Confer with the previous day's range. If your W1 analysis suggests expansion lower, you are looking for lower prices on the Daily range.
- **Candlestick Context:** If you are bearish, there is a greater advantage if yesterday’s candle was an **"up close"** candle. You want to see a move that breaks below yesterday’s low.
### **3. The Bearish Sell Setup**

- **The Sell Zone:** Your sell setup should form in the **lower quarter (25%) or lower third (33%)** of the previous day’s range. If you find yourself losing trades because you "chased" price, it is usually because you failed to refer back to this specific portion of the previous day's range.
- **Relative Equal Highs:** Inside this lower 1/3 or 1/4 area, look for **15m or 5m relative equal highs** (or a single high). We anticipate a future reaction here rather than reacting to current movement.
### **4. The Judas Swing Mechanics**

- **The Trap:** Between 2:00–4:00 AM, anticipate a rally **above** those relative equal highs.
- **Liquidity Run:** Resting above those highs are **buy stops**. The characteristic of London is to run up, enticing retail traders to chase the move long.
- **Smart Money Absorption:** In reality, this move provides the liquidity (buy orders) for **Smart Money to sell (short)**. It is a run on liquidity engineered to provide an entry mechanism for sophisticated traders.
- **Psychology:** This "Judas Swing" is often violent and feels like it will go up 100 pips. If you feel that way, you are **reacting**. You must remain disciplined, anticipating that price will reject those stops and move lower. [[21:47](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tub1JHNOIB0&t=1307)]
### **5. Execution and Mastery**

- **The Difficulty:** Selling short into buy stops is one of the hardest things to do because the move is "fast, furious, and scary."
- **Prediction:** Profitable trading is essentially **predicting the future** through anticipation, not reacting to the "now."
- **Price Level Application:** Using the Daily Range as a reference:
    - **100% (High):** 100
    - **50% (Equilibrium):** 60
    - **0% (Low):** 20
