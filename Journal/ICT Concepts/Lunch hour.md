### 1. The Purpose of the Lunch Hour (11:00 AM – 1:00 PM)

- **Liquidity Hunt:** This time window is specifically designed for the market to look for a "run on stops" that weren't hit during the morning session [[02:28:04](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tub1JHNOIB0&t=8884)].
- **Punishing Late Traders:** It targets individuals who have trailed their stop losses too tightly below old 15-minute lows (if bullish) or above old 15-minute highs (if bearish) [[02:25:31](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tub1JHNOIB0&t=8731)].
- **Clearing the Path:** The goal of the algorithm during this time is to remove "weak hands" before the afternoon (PM) session begins its primary move [[02:26:25](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tub1JHNOIB0&t=8785)].
### 2. Anticipation vs. Reaction
- **Stop Reacting:** ICT emphasizes that successful traders **anticipate** price movements rather than reacting to them. Reacting to lower time frame "knee-jerk" movements leads to chasing price and making emotional mistakes [[02:20:43](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tub1JHNOIB0&t=8443)].
- **Driving Analogy:** He compares trading to driving in rush hour. You shouldn't react to the drivers around you; you should anticipate their moves (like someone changing lanes without signaling) before they happen [[02:21:14](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tub1JHNOIB0&t=8474)].
### 3. Management of Winners
- **Psychological Toll:** Being in a profitable trade can be just as scary as being in a losing one if you don't understand the process. The "fear of what-if" often causes traders to close good positions too early [[02:24:38](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tub1JHNOIB0&t=8678)].
- **Gradual Progress:** He teaches that if a trade feels overwhelming, take partial profits (half off). If it still feels scary, close the whole thing. The goal is to remove yourself from "emotional turmoil" so you can manage risk effectively [[02:24:50](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tub1JHNOIB0&t=8690)].
### 4. The Path to Consistency

- **The "Solution":** There is no shortcut. Consistency is found through a specific sequence: **Back Testing** (conditioning yourself to see setups) $\rightarrow$ **Tape Reading** (watching without trading) $\rightarrow$ **Demo Trading** (for months) [[02:27:32](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tub1JHNOIB0&t=8852)].
- **Independence:** The goal of these time-based models is to make the trader independent of any mentor, including ICT himself, by using rule-based setups that don't require "guessing" [[02:28:13](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tub1JHNOIB0&t=8893)].
### 5. Rule-Based Trading
- **Simplification:** Traders should focus on a "unique model" within specific times of the day. This removes the need to worry about which "PDA" (Premium/Discount Array) or "Order Block" is better, as they are all equally effective when used within the right time parameters [[08:23](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tub1JHNOIB0&t=503)], [[02:28:04](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tub1JHNOIB0&t=8884)].
