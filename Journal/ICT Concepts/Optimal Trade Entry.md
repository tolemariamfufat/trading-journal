### Optimal Trade Entry (OTE)
- This is the "classic" model for traders who prefer Fibonacci levels over gaps.
- **The Setup:** After a Market Structure Shift and a strong expansion move.    
- **The Tool:** Pull a Fibonacci retracement from the Swing Low to the Swing High of that move.    
- **Entry:** Buy/Sell when price retraces into the **70.5%** level (the OTE).    
- **Stop Loss:** Below the 100% (the start of the move).
