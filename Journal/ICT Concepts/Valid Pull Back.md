

**Internal Pullbacks**
- **Bullish (Up)** A candle must break the LOW of the previous candle.
- **Bearish (Down)** A candle must break the HIGH of the previous candle.
- **Significance**: These are often used to identify "Inducement" or minor liquidity grabs that keep the trend moving without forming a major swing point.
**Structural Pullbacks**
- **Structural pullbacks** are the "big brothers" of internal pullbacks. These define the actual Swing Highs and Swing Lows of a market.
- **The Leg**: A structural pullback moves against the primary trend (the "impulse") and creates a distinct "V" or "A" shape on the chart.
- **The Break**: It only ends when the previous structural high or low is broken (a Break of Structure or BOS).
- **The Validation**: Once it hits 38.2%, it validates the internal structure and may become an area where "Inducement" is confirmed.
- **Significance**: If a structural pullback goes too far and breaks the starting point of the impulse, it’s no longer a pullback—it’s a Change of Character (CHoCH) or a trend reversal.
- **TYPES OF STRUCTURAL PULL BACK: AGRESSIVE, CORRECTIVE, SWEEP LIQUIDITY, MANIPULATION**
