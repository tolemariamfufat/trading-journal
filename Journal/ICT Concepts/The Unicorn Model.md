### The Unicorn Model
- This is widely considered one of the highest-probability setups because it uses two PD Arrays (Price Delivery Arrays) overlapping.
- The Setup: You look for a Breaker Block that has a Fair Value Gap (FVG) resting right inside it.
- The Logic: A Breaker Block is a failed Order Block. When price breaks through it with an FVG, it shows extreme institutional strength.
- Entry: Place your limit order at the point where the FVG and the Breaker overlap.
- Stop Loss: Just above/below the Breaker Block high/low.
