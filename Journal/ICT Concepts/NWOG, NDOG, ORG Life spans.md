## NWOG (New Week Opening Gap)Definition: The gap between Friday’s close (4:59 PM EST) and Sunday’s open (6:00 PM EST).
### Lifespan: 5 Weeks. Usage: You should keep the last 5 New Week Opening Gaps on your chart. They act as long-term institutional reference points for fair value. Even if price "fills" the gap, the levels (high, low, and midpoint) remain sensitive.
## NDOG (New Day Opening Gap)Definition: The gap between the previous day's settlement/close (typically 4:59 PM or 5:00 PM EST) and the new day's open (6:00 PM EST).
### Lifespan: 5 Days. Usage: You track the last 5 New Day Opening Gaps. These are crucial for intraday bias and identifying where price is likely to be "drawn" during the New York or London sessions.
## ORG (Opening Range Gap)Definition: The gap between the 4:15 PM EST close (Regular Trading Hours/RTH) and the 9:30 AM EST open the following morning.
### Lifespan: 3 Days. Usage: ICT suggests focusing on the ORGs from the last three trading days. These provide a tighter framework for immediate price delivery and are often used to find confluences for entries in the first hour of the New York session (9:30 AM – 10:30 AM).
