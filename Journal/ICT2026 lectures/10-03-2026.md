
- Unfilled fair value gap

- In this video, Inner Circle Trader (ICT) explains that when a specific candle (like the **10:07 candle** on the USTECH/Nasdaq) remains unfilled during a sell-side delivery, it serves as a significant marker of **market strength and institutional intent**.

Here is the breakdown of what it means when that candle remains unfilled:

- **Institutional Order Flow:** An unfilled candle, particularly a Fair Value Gap (FVG) or a specific liquidity void created at that time, indicates that the market is moving with such high conviction that it does not need to "reprice" back to that level.
    
- **Breadth and Momentum:** If the market continues to trade lower without returning to fill the 10:07 candle's range, it confirms that the sell-side move is aggressive. ICT often refers to this as "leaving a footprint" that smart money has no intention of revisiting in the immediate term.
    
- **Targeting Sell-Side Liquidity:** The fact that it remains unfilled suggests that the algorithm is prioritizing the reach for lower sell-side liquidity pools (Old Lows or Discount Arrays) rather than seeking internal range balance.
    
- **Market Symmetry:** ICT notes that if USTECH leaves these areas unfilled while other indices (like the S&P 500) behave differently, it can be used to spot **SMT Divergence**, further confirming the strength of the trend.
    

In summary, an unfilled 10:07 candle is a sign of **price displacement**. It suggests that the sell-side pressure is dominant and that the "path of least resistance" remains firmly to the downside.
