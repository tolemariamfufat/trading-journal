When Larry Williams speaks about Fundamentals, he isn't just talking about interest rates or GDP. To him, "Fundamentals" represent the unchanging laws of human nature and supply/demand that govern the movement of any speculative asset.

Whether you are trading onions in 1920 or Bitcoin in 2026, the "Reality" remains the same because the people (and the algorithms programmed by people) still react to the same two forces: Greed and Fear.
1. What are "The Fundamentals" in this Context?
In the Williams/ICT framework, "Fundamentals" can be broken down into three pillars:

- The Law of Scarcity (Supply & Demand): Price moves only when there is an imbalance. If there are "No More Sellers" at a 60-day low, the fundamental reality dictates that price must rise to find new sellers.

- The Law of Value: Markets move from Discount (Cheap) to Premium (Expensive). The "Fundamental" truth is that smart money buys when it is cheap and sells when it is expensive.

- The Business Cycle: Money flows in a circle. It moves from Cash → Commodities → Stocks → Bonds. This "Intermarket Relationship" is a fundamental law of how global capital is managed.

1. The Rules of Trading That Never Change
If the fundamentals are the "Physics," the Rules are the "Engineering." These are the constants that Larry Williams insists will be true 100 years from now:

A. The Rule of Expansion and Contraction (The Range)
Markets move from periods of Low Volatility (Small-Range Days) to High Volatility (Large-Range Days).

- The Constant: You cannot have a breakout without a prior consolidation.

- The Strategy: You use the 20/40/60-day study to find where volatility has been "quiet" for too long, knowing an explosion is a mathematical certainty.

B. The Rule of the "Judas" (The False Move)
To move a large amount of money, you must first create a "Reason" for the public to be on the wrong side.

- The Constant: Price almost always moves in the opposite direction of its true intent first to gather liquidity.

- The Strategy: This is why we watch the Opening Price. The "Fundamental" reality is that you need "Action" (Manipulation) to get "Reaction" (Displacement).

C. The Rule of Risk (The Survival Constant)
The market's job is to prove as many people wrong as possible.

- The Constant: Most trades will fail or underperform if held too long or not long enough.

- The Strategy: This is where Rules 2 and 4 come in ("If price falls much below the open, get out"). Limiting losses is a fundamental requirement because you cannot trade if you have no "bullets" left in your gun.

D. The Rule of Time (The Master Clock)
Human activity follows cycles (Daily, Weekly, Monthly).

- The Constant: Banks and institutions do not work 24/7. They have "Opening Bells" and "Closing Bells."

- The Strategy: Time is First. An explosive move at 10:00 AM NY Time is fundamentally different from a move at 10:00 PM because the "Power" (the participants) is different.
3. Dealing with the Reality
Larry’s advice to "learn to deal with this reality" means accepting that:

- You cannot predict the future, but you can identify a Momentum in Motion (Inertia).

- The market does not care about your opinion, only about where the Liquidity is.

- Profits take Time. You cannot force a "Large-Range Day" to happen faster than the clock allows.
